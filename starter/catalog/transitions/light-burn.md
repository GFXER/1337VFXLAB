A over-exposes into a warm white burn and B cools back out of it.

## How
ADJ over the cut: Exposure 0→+2.5 (5 f, ease-in) and back to 0 over 10 f (ease-out). Glow radius 120, intensity 0→1.6→0 on the same curve. Photo Filter warm 0→40→0 %. Cut on the brightest frame.
