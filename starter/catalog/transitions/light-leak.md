A warm orange leak sweeps across the frame and the cut hides under its brightest frame.

## How
Light-leak plate (or a soft orange Radial Gradient) in Screen, sweeping left→right over 18 f; opacity on a sine curve peaking at 100 % on the cut, plus +0.25 lift at the peak.
