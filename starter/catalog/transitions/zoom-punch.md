Punch into the face on A, land wide on B, with a 2-frame white kiss on the cut.

## How
ADJ Transform with the anchor on the face: scale 100→320 % (5 f, ease-in), then B 35→100 % (10 f, ease-out, influence 90 %). Shutter 300, Motion Tile mirror 300 %. White solid 55→0 % over 3 f on the cut.
