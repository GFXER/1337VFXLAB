A shockwave rings out from the face and the cut lands on its peak.

## How
Wave Warp / CC Ripple Pulse centred on the face: amplitude 0→28→0 px on a bell curve (±9 f around the cut), wavelength 26 px. 3 f crossfade at the peak, Gaussian Blur 0→3→0 with it.
