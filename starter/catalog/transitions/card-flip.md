The frame turns into a card, flips edge-on on the cut and comes back around as B.

## How
A and B precomped at 86 % on a dark background, 3D layers, camera 1100 px. Y rotation 0→−90° on A (5 f, ease-in), +90→0° on B (10 f, ease-out). Darken with the angle (Exposure −1 when edge-on). Motion blur on.
