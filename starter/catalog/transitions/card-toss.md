A shrinks into a rounded card with a soft shadow and gets tossed off the top; B is already underneath.

## How
A: scale 100→62 %, rounded-corner mask 0→70 px, rotation 0→−6° (8 f expo-out), then Y −1050 px and rotation −10° in 5 f ease-in. Drop Shadow 55 %, softness 60. B underneath settles 108→100 % over 22 f. Motion blur on.
